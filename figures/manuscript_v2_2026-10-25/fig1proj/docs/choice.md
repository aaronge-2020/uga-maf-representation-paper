# Choice — Concept A (horizontal pipeline), with fixes

Both kept concepts were drafted and rendered at 300 dpi.

Concept B (vertical) failed on its own terms: the fork lines strike the
"Feature tables" heading and the Burden column, the MuAt frame border crosses
the "Signatures + Bio MAF" name, the MuAt return line runs the full width of
the figure under the endpoints box, and the "Same five test groups" label
lands outside every box it could belong to. Its one advantage, wider glyphs,
does not survive those collisions.

Concept A reads in the paper's own order and gives the fairness claim a single
object (the test-group strip) that both paths touch. It is chosen, with four
fixes found in its draft:
1. The convergence wires struck through the four count labels. Counts move up
   onto the name line, right-aligned in their own column, clear of the wires.
2. The MAF table's "Alleles" and "Gene" headers touched. Columns widened.
3. The models frame was half empty and its tuning note sat outside it. The
   pills move down and the note moves inside the frame, under the pills.
4. Glyph column shifts right 6 pt to match the widened MAF table and keep the
   fork trunk clear of the table edge.
